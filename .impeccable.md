## Design Context

### Users

Technically curious readers, friends, and peers visit to understand who Sreyas is and to browse his writing. The interface should make wandering between notes, subjects, and dates feel immediate without competing with the work itself.

### Brand Personality

Precise, editorial, and quietly playful. The site should feel authored by a technically exacting person with taste: intelligent without posturing, personal without becoming precious, and calm without becoming generic.

### Aesthetic Direction

A restrained editorial index with subtle instrument-panel logic. Literata carries the literary voice; Geist Mono and Iosevka handle compact navigational metadata. Cool, ink-tinted neutrals and a single blue accent work in both light and dark themes. Rules, type, and deliberate alignment establish hierarchy; controls may interrupt or terminate those rules when their geometry communicates structure. Avoid generic dashboard cards, oversized rounded pills, glass effects, ornamental gradients, and UI-library-default layouts.

### Design Principles

1. Let writing lead; navigation should be compact, obvious, and satisfying to use.
2. Make geometry meaningful: align controls to rules, baselines, and content edges instead of placing them approximately.
3. Prefer editorial typography and open lists over repeated cards and containers.
4. Make every word earn its place; avoid repeating visible state or adding twee helper copy to self-explanatory controls.
5. Treat hover, focus, dark mode, and reduced motion as first-class states with clear contrast and no surprising color shifts.
