Compilation of all my fav **_<span style="font-family: 'Comic Sans MS', 'Comic Sans', cursive, sans-serif; color: red !important;">nerdy</span>_** YT channels; ranges from physics I can half-understand to memes/reactions on new tech framework drops.

I've broken them into sections, and *some* of these sections have subsections. Every channel has its own unique personality and vibes, but I try to organize things when it’s appropriate.

### Tech, development, CS, programming

<br />

##### Leaning towards web dev

<MarginNote>his career used to be backend i think; nowadays the channel is AI-infected (at the same time, it still has really cool info)</MarginNote>
- **Theo \- t3.gg:** nowadays quite more popular, streamer personality-ish, makes lots of different types of web dev-related videos like framework insiders and reactions/roasts to new releases; has good knowledge of finance and investing; does lots of collabs with/talks about people from all around tech community

##### More general, miscellaneous

- **Primeagen:** does web dev, talks about Rust, Go, web dev, HTMX (never tried), other stuff; he's also shorter than Theo.
- **Fireship:** meme-oriented, funny, but info-packed and practical, "daily code report", 100s of \[X\], really fast-paced
<MarginNote>if you haven't already seen this, it's really good to start with if you want inspiration for some projects</MarginNote>
- **Sebastian Lague:** really interesting projects; posts long videos (typically within \~1 hr) about all development; from CFD and chess to game dev; uses C\# and Unity. 100% rec. 
- **Zed Industries:** develop the Zed IDE in Rust; really cool "Zed Decoded" videos on inner-workings of their IDE
- **Vercidium:** game engine from scratch
- **Surfshark Academy:** YT shorts
- **GOTO conferences:** some are good, I don't think I would suggest binging a random one though
- **Daedalus Community:** only halfway-watched his series on functional programming
- **Code Aesthetic:** goated, pls watch.
- **Malloc:** C++ learning series, by same guy of Code Aesthetic
- **Dreams of Code:** very nice video quality, 2026 is his year of Rust (he’s working on a video-editing app), and he inspired a lot of my current setup for Nix and daily terminal work.

##### Cybersecurity-ish?

- **Pwn Function:** explains some hacks, some web dev-related and some other low-level stuff

##### Low-level, Rust, C++, systems programming

- **Low Level:** okay for some videos
- **No Boilerplate:** fast, technical videos, mainly about Rust and programming but also has a sci-fi podcast that I've never listened to.
- **Jon Gjengset:** very informational and educational, but can sometimes be very long & technical; Rust and low-level
- **fasterthanlime:** goated, sometimes really technical, but goated
- **Rusty Bits:** embedded Rust
- **Logan Smith:** amazing guy; really insightful videos, often comparing to C++

##### UI \+ UX \+ etc

- **Juxtopposed:** good; I have this tendency to watch UI/UX videos when I'm bored
- **Tailwind Labs:** subscribe to [this](https://tailwindcss.com/build-uis-that-dont-suck) and watch there.
- tons of other ones I haven't subscribed to; you'll know when you find a good one 😁

##### CS, DSA-specific

- **Spanning Tree:** good, nice and clear animations
- **NeetCode:** guy who made [neetcode.io](http://neetcode.io), a way of getting better at leetcode
- **Computerphile:** lots of things tbh

##### Systems Design

- **ByteByteGo** and **Arjay McCandless** (YT shorts)

### Physics

- **PBS Spacetime:** really shallow but glosses over really advanced material
- **Dr. Jorge S. Diaz:** insanely technical but love his German accent and ability to connect things; has a unique historical take which furthers his ability to make connections (what I wish the *Oppenheimer* movie and physics in mass media was/is like); can barely half-understand his videos; tell myself to get back to binging this man soon after locking in physics soon
- **Physics for the Birds:** love this dude so much; must watch his video on the "Lights out" problem and maybe the one on Turing patterns; other videos are roughly decent.
- **Richard Behiel:** I wish I could understand, but it's almost always far too technical; will need to understand one day
- **ScienceClic:** good animations and really good explanations
- **Lectures by Walter Lewin:** watched part of his E\&M course—they were pretty good; not really a fan of his personality outside of his lectures and other things
- **Gabriele Carcassi:** can't understand any of his videos, but the main project he's working on actively is really cool and that's why I'm staying
- **AlphaPhoenix:** good, some mind-bending videos change your perspective a lot
- **Real Engineering:** I guess this is physics? I just watch this when I'm bored
- **Steve Mould:** fluids \+ tensegrity \+ unintuitive torpedo \+ other fun physics. classic
- **eigenchris:** used to watch a couple of his SR/GR videos, it's been a while now
- **minutephysics:** yes. a classic.
- **Overview Effekt**: astrophysics-themed, often goes over topics in very interesting depth from science-fiction movies like Project Hail Mary, Star Trek, etc
